# v1.4 六张自动被动卡

内置 ImageGen 使用现有卡面 `public/art/spell-6.webp`、`public/art/spell-11.webp` 作为质感参考。原始图像位于 `art/forge/cards/cards-master.png`，本目录留有 1536×1024 母版 `cards-master.png`。

母版为 3×2 等宽方格，分界位于水平 512、1024 以及竖直 512 像素。每格四边各收 2 像素，Lanczos 缩至 384 方，编码为质量 92 的 WebP。按顺序对应镜砂分身、日轮焰盘、渡魂灯、牵引锚、霜羽飞梭、蓄电战鼓。使用支持 WebP 的 Pillow 运行 `python3 rebuild.py` 可从本地母版重建六个成品与 48 像素对照图。

## 提示词

Use case: stylized-concept. Deliver one LANDSCAPE 3:2 atlas of six precisely tiled square collectible skill-card illustrations for an original premium 3D cartoon western-fantasy arena game. THREE COLUMNS, TWO ROWS, exact equal squares, full bleed edge to edge in every tile, no separator, margin, typography, border, frames, badge, UI, watermark or caption. References are previous collectible spell card art. Match their SEMI-CHUNKY SCULPTURAL MATERIAL, dramatic cinematic warm key light, strong contrast, saturated candy jewel tones, crisp beveled 3D forms, rich shadows and graphic radial backgrounds. Each tile has ONE very readable central larger-than-life icon with enough simple visual space for 48px display, subject fills 75–88% of cell. Distinct color key and unmistakable silhouette. Each cell stays strictly inside its own square. New subjects in fixed exact order, all ORIGINAL, no reference object's design to be repeated:
Top left: "Mirror Sand Double" – a sly small BLUE-GREEN DESERT FOX apparition leaping to the right in a prancing side pose, huge triangular ears, pointed face and a single swooping tail turning into jade sand crystals. Behind fox one faint shimmering translucent echo silhouette to make a magical mirage duplicate. Teal and mint highlights over dark cyan sandstorm, cute desert animal fantasy, one main readable fox. Do not generate a human or knife.
Top middle: "Solar Halo Wheel" – one thick golden-orange FLAMING SUN WHEEL, oblique three-quarter view, shape is an actual open donut halo with an opening, bright gold metal rim, white-hot flame thorns sweeping in ONE circular direction around the outer rim, heat and swirling orange sparks, cinnabar red backdrop. Distinct single golden wheel.
Top right: "Ferryman's Soul Lantern" – hanging hexagonal dark brass hand lantern with a short dramatic curved handle above, magenta glass, imprisoned swirling violet-pink SOUL FLAME with two expressive pale eyes inside, silhouettes clear. Small purple-pink wisps trailing inward, glowing rim light on dark burgundy-purple backdrop. Clearly a lantern, no moth.
Bottom left: "Tractor Anchor" – one chunky mechanical brass iron ANCHOR with two large stocky hooked flukes, a short length of heavy chain attached to the eye, a turquoise round rivet, shown diagonally and filling cell on rich deep petrol-teal background with dusty gold motion rays. Clearly readable anchor silhouette, not a hook alone.
Bottom middle: "Frostfeather Fan" – precisely THREE ice-blue feathered darts displayed diagonally in a sharp open hand fan with tips outward to upper right, glossy sapphire shafts, icy crystal arrowheads and frosty white plume fletching, jewel highlights against ultramarine blue/cyan backdrop. Readable feather-arrow silhouette distinct from a snowflake.
Bottom right: "Thunder Drum" – a chunky purple royal WAR DRUM, round cylindrical dark indigo body and bright taut lavender drumhead, thick yellow brass rims and rope lacing, one small mallet striking upper rim, a bold violet lightning bolt flashes from the drumhead. Three-quarter overhead view, vibrant purple-white arcs with dark plum backdrop, inspiring rhythm and electric charge. Must look like an actual DRUM, not a power coil. Exactly six separate square artworks, no text characters anywhere.
