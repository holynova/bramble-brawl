## lobby.webp

`source-images/exec-ee6cbe7a-d786-42b5-b825-1ae666a47540.png`

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset: high-end 16:9 widescreen environmental matte for a desert brawler hero selection screen, original stylized Brawl Stars meets Clash Royale 3D game key art. Lush cinematic stylized 3D sculpted shapes, insanely polished animation feature still with simple blocky expressive forms, vivid clean colours, soft painted shadows. Dramatic band of rich saturated turquoise blue sky with apricot horizontal cumulus clouds over epic peach red-rock mesas and lavender distant mountains, a golden mine and rickety railway to the left, crooked wood water tower and frontier saloon at the far right, foreground cactus silhouette and jagged wooden fence on far edges. The central 40% is a calm spectacular atmospheric basin, in the lower center a flat gently sunlit dusty cliff landing where character art and menus will be composited. Dramatic BUT balanced, long shadows, directional desert sunset, jewel greens against sunstruck golden terra-cotta / vermillion, shadowed ground in bottom corners, huge sense of depth. Visual storytelling and gigantic playful shapes, not detailed busy grain. No people, no creatures, no text, no signs with letters, no logos, no UI. Landscape panorama, precise environment concept art full bleed.

重建命令 `cwebp -q 90 -alpha_q 100 -m 6 "source-images/exec-ee6cbe7a-d786-42b5-b825-1ae666a47540.png" -o "public/art/lobby.webp"`
