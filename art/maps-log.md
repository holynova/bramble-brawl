Distribute these attractive medium-size patches in ALL four quadrants AS WELL AS THE DEAD CENTER. Use asymmetric perspective-correct sweeping natural arrangements and group details, separated by quick resting spaces no larger than one quarter width. Ground remains navigable, especially all of center: no large standalone scenery, barricades, chasms, big trees or poles in central 75%. Props and mine cliffs are only at the extreme outer 10-12% edge, not a continuous framing border, some map edges go off-screen naturally. Graphic areas, less noise, avoid an empty circle in the middle, avoid oversaturated highlights or overbusy props that outcompete combatants. No units, no characters, no letters, no compass, no grid, no typography, no UI, no border. Theme: HIGH NOON GOLD QUARRY, rich warm apricot golden terra cotta floor, sunlit vermillion canyon. A playful open Western gold-mine clearing with meandering twin tracks snaking across and through the center on a diagonal continuing to edge, a light sandy bootprint path branching through middle, pebbles and flat round stones, dust fans, scattered copper-colored leaves, short knee-high mint-green grass in organic splashes and a few tiny yellow daisies. Contrast emerald/sage grass with warmly saturated orange ground. Outer edges have chunky red sandstone, at far upper left a wooden mine entrance tucked into outer cliff with gold nuggets and mine props, near upper right a tiny turquoise basin, vibrant red-flowering prickly pears, at lower right a little red-roof mine hut next to the edge. Distinct clear rich warm daytime light from upper left. High artistic quality and easy player readability. Center has useful mottled ground cover and compressed mineral layers.
